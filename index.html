Harmonisation & Funding Review

A defined, employer-side review

A review of how health insurance is arranged across countries, where better coordination may help, and which funding or procurement decisions deserve closer examination.

The starting point

We begin with the decision your organisation needs to make.

An initial conversation establishes four things:

1
Countries and employer entities
Where the programme operates and who the employer is in each place.
2
Employee or insured-member groups
Who is covered, from local employees to assignees and travellers.
3
Programme ownership
Who owns the programme and who buys each policy.
4
The next material renewal or decision
The date and decision the review should inform.
What an agreed review can examine
  • Current policies, insurers, benefit designs, eligibility and geographic coverage
  • Renewal timing, purchasing responsibility and areas of fragmentation or duplication
  • Premiums, claims experience and administration, considered on their respective bases
  • Coordination with travel insurance, assistance and Global Mobility arrangements
  • Whether consolidation or different funding structures merit further feasibility work
